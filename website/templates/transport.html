TLC Trip Record Data

Green taxi trip records include pickup and drop-off dates and times, pickup and drop-off location codes, trip distances, itemized fares, rate types, payment types, and passenger counts. The files in this research collection follow the field structure of the TLC trip record archive.

This website is a research replica. All downloadable records have been generated for this collection and are not actual taxi journeys. The archive contains January, February, and March 2023, with 240 records in each monthly extract.

Download the original Parquet files before beginning your analysis. The synthetic observations may contain data-quality issues; they should not be used as factual evidence about trips or fares.
